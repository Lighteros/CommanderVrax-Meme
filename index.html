01 About

The officer who holds the corridor.

The bridge stays dark until the tubes come up. Commander Vrax stands in that light: a round cream face, a black collar, Earth curved in the glass behind the watch.

VRAX is the call sign. The rank is Commander-in-Chief of Robinhood. Orders clear on Robinhood Chain, through Uniswap, and the public board is DexScreener.

02 How to buy

Four moves. Then you are on the deck.

VRAX trades on Robinhood Chain. Match the contract posted on X before you sign anything.

  1. 01

    Add the chain

    In an EVM wallet, add Robinhood Chain by hand. The chain ID is the part that matters.

  2. 02

    Fund the gas

    Hold ETH on Robinhood Chain. Swaps and approvals spend a little of it.

  3. 03

    Swap on Uniswap

    Set the network to Robinhood Chain and swap ETH for VRAX. Read the contract from the post on X and match it character for character.

  4. 04

    Watch the board

    Open DexScreener on the Robinhood chain and follow the VRAX pair from the bridge.
